Enterprise Capabilities

Intelligent Attendance Infrastructure

Engineered for high accuracy, lightning throughput, and zero proxy loopholes across campus and enterprise spaces.

AI Face Analysis

Advanced neural networks recognize every student's face from a single class photo in milliseconds, making group attendance instant and accurate.

Sequential Voice ID

Students say "Present" one-by-one, and our audio-AI matches their unique voice biometrics against stored 256-d embeddings in real-time.

QR-Driven Roster

Course codes generate unique, encrypted QR codes for instant student enrollment. No manual typing or data entry headaches.

Anti-Spoofing & Liveness

Deep anti-spoofing algorithms detect photo printouts, screen displays, and voice recordings, ensuring only live, physically present attendees are registered.

Supabase Realtime Sync

PostgreSQL with Row Level Security guarantees instant cross-device updates for faculty, administration, and students without manual synchronization.

Audit-Ready Reports

Generate one-click CSV and PDF reports formatted to accreditation guidelines with confidence scores, exact check-in timestamps, and summary analytics.
